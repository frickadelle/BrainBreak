# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Colleagues take a short shared break during a weekly team meeting. They use laptops and phones, sometimes in German and sometimes in English. Rooms support two to thirty people. A pair can play together; larger groups can compete in teams.

## Product Purpose

Brainbreak is a browser multiplayer game with room invitations, a shared category vote, six synchronized rounds, clear reveals and persistent weekly points. Players should reach a real game quickly, understand their current role and receive reliable feedback. Timers, guesses, scoring and progress are owned by the server.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, playful and calm. Keep the existing illustrated brain as the recognizable visual anchor. The user explicitly likes the brain and requests a more minimal interface around it.

## Anti-references

The previous interface's oversized promotional headings, repeated slogans, multicolour card treatments, decorative orbit effects and heavy elevation compete with the actual game. Reduce those features while preserving the brain illustration and readable game states.

## Design Principles

- Put creating, joining and playing ahead of promotional content.
- Preserve the brain illustration; let surrounding elements be quiet.
- Show one clear next action and the information needed to take it.
- Give every player a consistent deadline, role and answer status.
- Explain team roles, public drawing chat and mixed rounds in ordinary language.
- Keep feedback sounds short, gentle and optional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain keyboard controls, visible focus, labelled inputs, adequate contrast and responsive phone layouts. Status must use text or icons as well as colour. Support reduced motion. A sound toggle must persist; visual feedback must remain sufficient when muted. No specific conformance level or additional accommodation has been requested. User-entered names and clues remain user content.
